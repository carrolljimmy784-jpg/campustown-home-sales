College Town Housing Explorer

## What this site is for

This site compares housing markets across major U.S. college towns using 35,442 home sales records. It allows users to explore median sale prices, compare cities and universities, and understand how housing markets differ near university campuses. Some features include median sale price by university, city, sale price distribution, and a feature that allows you to search by city to explore # of sales, average distance to campus, and median/average sale price.

## Data notes

Sales below $10,000 were excluded from all price-based calculations because the dataset documentation identifies them as implausible or non-market transactions. Median sale prices are emphasized because the dataset contains extreme outliers, including sales above $5,000,000. Any data about home type, addresses, or days on market were left unanlayzed due to inaccuracies or inconsistent/missing data.
