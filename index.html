Data notes

Sales below $10,000 are excluded from all price calculations, including the overall and city median sale prices. The sales analyzed count includes only rows with a valid numeric sale price of at least $10,000.

About the data

Each row represents a completed home sale. City and university counts reflect the qualifying sales included in this dashboard.

Data: College-town home sales dataset, version 1.0 (2026). Values shown are calculated from the source data.
